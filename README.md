## 📌 Project Overview

A modern, responsive corporate website developed for **Refilwe Thapelo Trading & Projects (Pty) Ltd**.

The website provides the company with a professional digital presence and showcases its business activities, services, projects, brands and contact information.

The website was designed with a clean, modern and responsive interface to ensure a professional experience across desktop and mobile devices.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- Responsive corporate website
- Modern navigation
- Business directory
- Business information pages
- Project showcase
- Interactive project details
- Business detail views
- Client inquiry functionality
- Contact section
- Responsive mobile design
- Custom business imagery
- Company branding and logos
